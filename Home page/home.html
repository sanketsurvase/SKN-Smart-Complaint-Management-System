How It Works

Step Process
1 Click on Register Complaint and enter your complaint details.
2 Select your department and complaint category, then submit the complaint.
3 After successful submission, the system displays a confirmation animation and generates a unique Tracking ID.
4 Save or copy your Tracking ID for future use.
5 Open Check Complaint Status and enter your Tracking ID to view the current complaint status.
