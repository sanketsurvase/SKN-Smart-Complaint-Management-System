Step-by-Step Lifecycle

How the Grievance Redressal System Operates

A structured, 5-stage lifecycle ensuring every student issue is logged, acted upon, and verified.

01

Lodge Complaint

Click Register Complaint. Select your department, enter incident details, attach optional photo evidence, and choose anonymous or identified filing.

Takes < 2 minutes
02

AI Processing & Triage

The smart AI system analyzes the description to assign urgency level, verify appropriate departmental routing, and alerts the designated resolver.

Instant automated routing
03

Tracking ID Generation

A unique cryptographic Tracking ID (e.g., CMP2608230001) is generated. Save this token to follow progress anytime without needing login credentials.

Auto-saved & copyable
04

Resolver Action & Audit

The department resolver inspects the physical site or administrative issue, performs corrective maintenance, and logs action-taken remarks.

On-ground action
05

HOD Verification & Closure

The Head of Department reviews the resolution quality before formally closing the ticket. The student views the updated status as Resolved.

Verified closure
