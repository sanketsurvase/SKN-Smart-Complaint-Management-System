Step-by-Step Guide

How The Redressal Process Works

From anonymous registration to verified resolution, our streamlined 5-step lifecycle ensures total transparency.

01

Lodge Grievance

Click on Register Complaint and enter the department, category, campus location, and description.

02

Smart Routing

The system intelligently routes the ticket directly to the designated department HOD and category resolver.

03

Get Tracking ID

Upon submission, a unique Tracking ID (e.g. CMP...) is generated. Copy and save it for future tracking.

04

Ground Resolution

The assigned technical team inspects the reported issue on site and initiates resolution measures.

05

Track & Complete

Check the live progress via Check Status anytime. The ticket is marked resolved once inspected.
