Frequently Asked Questions

How long does resolution typically take?

Most campus maintenance issues (electrical, plumbing, IT) are inspected within 24 to 48 working hours. Complex structural repairs may take longer.

What if I lost my Tracking ID?

Because the portal is 100% anonymous, student names are never linked to tickets. If you lose your tracking ID, please re-submit the complaint.

Who monitors these complaints?

All complaints are actively supervised by the respective Engineering Department HOD and assigned to certified campus technicians.

Can I register another grievance?

Yes. You can lodge as many grievances as required through the Register Complaint page.
